Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · effective 26 September 2026 · applies to DrinkBy for iPhone. The seller name shown on the App Store product page is the contracting party for this purchase.

The short version. DrinkBy has no server. Your cellar — label photos, dates, notes, prices — is written to the app's own storage inside your iPhone and never leaves it. We have no database of you to sell, leak or hand over, because there is nothing to hold.

What we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address required to use the app.
  • No analytics or usage telemetry. The app contains no tracking code and no third-party SDKs.
  • No advertising, and therefore no advertising identifier.
  • No location.
  • No wine list, cellar contents, label image or note is uploaded anywhere. There is no endpoint to upload to.

Label reading

When you scan a label, the image is processed on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition (Vision), and — where the device supports it — Apple's on-device language model, to suggest values for producer, vintage, alcohol and bottle size. Neither step sends your image or the recognised text to us or to anyone else. If the model is unavailable on your device, the fields are parsed from the printed text instead, and the app tells you so on screen rather than pretending otherwise.

Reading a label works with no network connection. That is deliberate: cellars, winery basements and trains are where this happens.

What is stored on your device

  • Your wine records: producer, name, vintage, region, grape, country, alcohol, bottle size, quantity, purchase price and currency, purchase date, drinking-window dates, tasting notes, bin code, and the label photo you took.
  • Your opening log: which bottle you marked as opened, and when.
  • Whether the unlimited-cellar unlock is active.

All of it lives in the app's sandboxed storage on that iPhone (Application Support). It is not backed up to any DrinkBy service, because there is none. iCloud backup behaviour is governed by Apple's normal rules for app documents, not by us.

Purchases

Unlocking the unlimited cellar is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We receive only the transaction information that StoreKit exposes to the app on your device, which is what lets the app stay unlocked when you reinstall it. We never see your name, address, card details or receipt email, and nothing renews: there is no subscription in this product.

Permissions

  • Camera — used for one job: photographing a label you choose to scan.
  • Photo Library (only if you tap "Choose a photo you already took") — the selected image is read into memory, recognised on-device, and not sent anywhere.

Both are optional. You can use the app entirely by typing bottles in by hand.

Your data, your hands

Settings → Export everything (CSV) writes the whole cellar to a file you can open in Numbers or Excel and share wherever you like. Settings → Delete all wines erases the records immediately, with no confirmation round-trip to us. Deleting the app removes everything else it holds.

Children

DrinkBy is a wine cellar tool and is rated for ages 17 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the version and date at the top of this page change, and the same page is updated. The in-app copy of the policy (Settings → Privacy) is kept in step with it. Continued use after an update means you accept the updated terms; in practice, a policy that promises "we hold nothing" has very little room to grow.
